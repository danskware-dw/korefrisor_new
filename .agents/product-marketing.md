# Product marketing context — FrisørHjem

## Business
- **Name:** FrisørHjem
- **Type:** Mobile / home-visit hairdresser (udekørende frisør), not a walk-in salon
- **Base:** Kastrup (2770), Denmark — service radius ~30 km
- **Audience:** Elderly / mobility-limited adults, children under 12, relatives booking for family, care homes
- **Language:** Danish (da-DK); some English keywords for AI/search

## Offer
Bookable: **Klip** (350 kr), **Pensionistklip** (325 kr), **Børneklip** (225 kr).  
Add-ons: skæg, pandehår, bryn. Do not add colour/balayage.  
Care-home visits: N residents, one travel fee, one invoice, recurring weekday.  
Travel: free within 5 km of Kastrup; zoned fees after; map + locked total. Extra person: 50 kr off, travel once.  
Pay with MobilePay at booking, or invoice to relative. Standing schedule every 4/6/8 weeks. SMS the day before.

## Primary SEO / AI queries
- hjemmeklip Kastrup / Amager / Tårnby / Dragør
- udekørende frisør / mobil frisør København
- pensionistklip hjemme / frisør til ældre
- børneklip hjemme
- frisør der kommer hjem til dig

## Conversion goals
1. Online booking (`/book`)
2. Phone call
3. Relatives understand how to book (`/for-parorende`)

## Constraints
- Large type, ~48px tap targets (elderly-friendly)
- No invented reviews/testimonials
- Placeholder `[RET DETTE]` fields must be filled before real deploy (phone, email, street, name, siteUrl, CVR)
- Do not invent Google Business Profile content; operator must claim GBP

## Competitive angle
Quiet home visits vs salon travel; clear price before confirm; senior-friendly pacing; relatives can book.
